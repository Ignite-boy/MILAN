FAQ

Decentralized social network questions

What is a decentralized social network?

It is a social platform architecture designed so identity, data, permissions or other core components are not controlled entirely by one centralized service.

How does a decentralized social network work?

A decentralized social network can separate identity and data from the application itself. In MILAN, DID is used for the identity layer and DWN architecture for the data layer.

What is a DWN?

DWN stands for Decentralized Web Node. In the MILAN model it acts as a user-centered data layer for records and data access.

What is a DID?

DID stands for Decentralized Identifier. It is a decentralized identity mechanism designed to reduce dependence on a traditional centralized identity provider.

Is MILAN a decentralized social network?

MILAN positions itself as a privacy-first decentralized social network built around DID and DWN architecture and permission-aware sharing.

How is MILAN related to Web5?

MILAN uses Web5-oriented concepts including decentralized identity and DWN architecture as part of its social model.
