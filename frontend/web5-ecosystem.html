05 — FAQ

Understand the Milan model.

Is Milan a social media platform?

Social and communication features can be part of Milan, but Milan's broader direction is a Web5 ecosystem built around identity, data, privacy, permission and digital ownership.

What role does DID play?

A DID represents the decentralized identity layer in the Web5 model. It is intended to make digital identity more independent of a single platform.

What role does DWN play?

DWN refers to Decentralized Web Node architecture. In the Milan vision, it represents a user-centered data layer for storing and accessing digital information.

Can people earn from their data?

Milan's long-term direction includes exploring transparent, consent-based digital value models where people can participate in value created from their digital presence. Specific mechanisms should only be described as available when they are actually implemented.
