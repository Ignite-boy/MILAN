07 · FAQ

Questions about the Milan ecosystem.

What is Milan becoming?

Milan is being developed as a Web5 ecosystem where identity, personal data, privacy, permission and connected digital experiences form a common product foundation.

Is Milan only a decentralized social network?

No. Social is an important experience, but it is one part of a broader ecosystem that can include media, AI-assisted tools, knowledge and future services.

Why are DID and DWN important?

DID represents the decentralized identity layer and DWN represents the decentralized data layer that support the people-first architecture Milan is exploring.

Does the ecosystem already include every future feature?

No. Some parts of the vision are implemented today and others are part of Milan's longer-term direction. The page distinguishes the ecosystem vision from individual product availability.
