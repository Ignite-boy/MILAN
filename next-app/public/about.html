THE IDEA

Social media should not require you to give up control.

MILAN is built around a simple product principle: identity, data and sharing should be understandable parts of the system, not invisible platform-side decisions.

01
◉

Private by default

Posts can begin private and be shared publicly or with selected people according to the application's permission model.

02
ID

Decentralized identity

A DID provides the identity layer so the social experience is not defined only by a traditional platform account.

03

Decentralized data

DWN architecture provides a user-centered data layer for records, media and permissions.

04

No ad-first model

MILAN's product direction is centered on the social experience, rather than building the interface around behavioral advertising.

Founder of MILAN
