Who Is the Founder of MILAN?

Nitesh Pandey is the founder and creator of MILAN. MILAN is a privacy-first decentralized social network focused on user-owned data, decentralized identity (DID), Decentralized Web Nodes (DWN), and data privacy.

Who founded MILAN?

MILAN was founded by Nitesh Pandey. He created MILAN as a decentralized social media platform at milanlife.in.

What is MILAN?

MILAN is a decentralized social network where the product architecture is centered on data ownership, privacy, DWN technology and DID-based identity.

MILAN, DWN and DID

MILAN uses Decentralized Web Node (DWN) and Decentralized Identifier (DID) concepts as part of its decentralized social-network architecture. Learn more about Web5, DWN and DID or explore decentralized social networking.
