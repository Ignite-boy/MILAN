Social Media Privacy Guide 2026 — Own Your Data

Most "free" social apps are paid for with your data. This guide explains what actually gets tracked, the quick settings that reduce it, and the structural fix — networks where your data lives in your own node.

How do social media apps track you?

Centralized platforms log what you post, like, watch, search and share; who you talk to and how often; your location patterns, contacts and device details. This profile powers ad targeting. Even end-to-end encrypted chats still produce metadata — who messaged whom, when — which is valuable on its own.

Quick wins: settings you can change today

  1. Turn off ad personalisation in every app's settings.
  2. Cut app permissions — location, contacts, microphone — to "only while using" or off.
  3. Don't use "Login with Facebook/Google" on third-party sites; it links your activity across the web.
  4. Make accounts private and audit old posts.
  5. Download your data export once — see for yourself how much they hold.
