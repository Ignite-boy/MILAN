About MILAN

Social media where you actually own your data.

MILAN is a privacy-first decentralized social network. Instead of storing your life on a company's servers, every member gets their own Decentralized Web Node (DWN) and a cryptographic Decentralized Identifier (DID) — so your posts, media and connections stay yours.

What makes MILAN different

Mainstream social platforms are built around surveillance: they own your content, profile you, and monetize your attention. MILAN flips that model. Your data lives in storage you control, sharing is consent-based, and there are no ads, no tracking, and no data selling.

🔒
Private by default

Every post starts private. You decide what becomes public or gets shared with specific people.

🆔
You own your identity

A Decentralized Identifier (DID) is yours alone — no platform sits between you and your presence.

🗄
Your own node

Each account is backed by a real Decentralized Web Node, so your content is self-sovereign.

🚫
No ad machine

MILAN doesn't profile you or sell your data. The product is the network, not you.

How MILAN works

  1. You get a DIDWhen you join, MILAN creates a cryptographic identity that belongs to you.
  2. Your DWN is provisionedA personal Decentralized Web Node becomes the home for your posts, media and permissions.
  3. You share on your termsKeep things private, post publicly, or share with selected DIDs — your choice, every time.
  4. You stay in controlBecause the data is signed by your identity and lives in your node, you keep ownership.

Built on Web5: DWN + DID

Learn about Web5, DWN and DID, discover decentralized social media, read the privacy guide, or explore MILAN as a private social network.

MILAN uses Decentralized Web Node technology so posts, media and sharing permissions live in storage you own. Decentralized Identifiers let you prove who you are without handing personal data to a central authority. Together they form the foundation of a genuinely privacy-first social network.
