MILAN is a privacy-first decentralized social media platform where you own your data. Instead of one company storing everything about you, every MILAN member gets their own Decentralized Web Node (DWN) and a Decentralized Identifier (DID). No ads. No tracking. No data selling.

What is decentralized social media?

On a normal social network, your posts, photos, friends and private messages live inside one company's central servers. They control it, mine it, and sell access to advertisers. Decentralized social media flips this: your data lives in a node that belongs to you. On MILAN, that node is a real DWN, secured by your own cryptographic identity (DID). You decide what is private, what is public, and who can see what.

Why MILAN?

  • You own your data — stored in your isolated DWN, not a central database.
  • Private by default — share publicly or with selected people only when you choose.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data selling — your attention is not the product.
  • Self-sovereign identity (DID) — a portable identity that you control.
  • Real-time and modern — fast feed, media, themes, music and more.
  • Made in India — built for a privacy-conscious new generation.
