Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026
The short version: MILAN uses only what's needed to keep you signed in and remember your settings. No advertising cookies. No third-party trackers. Nothing follows you around the web.

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Both can be used for good (keeping you logged in) or bad (tracking you across the internet). MILAN uses them only for the first kind.

2. What we use

TypePurposeCategory
Authentication tokenKeeps you signed in securely to your account (JWT)Strictly necessary
Preferences (local storage)Remembers your theme, language and app settings on your deviceStrictly necessary / functional
Service worker cache (PWA)Makes the app load fast and work offlineStrictly necessary

3. What we don't use

  • No advertising or ad-targeting cookies (no Google AdSense, no DART cookies, no ad networks).
  • No third-party analytics trackers building profiles of you.
  • No cross-site tracking, fingerprinting or web beacons.
  • No sale or sharing of cookie data — there is none to sell.

Because we use only strictly necessary storage, MILAN does not need an EU-style cookie consent banner — there is nothing optional to consent to.

4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. Note that clearing MILAN's data will sign you out and reset your saved preferences, since that is all we store.

5. Changes

If we ever add optional cookies (for example privacy-respecting analytics), we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or data? Email support@milanlife.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
